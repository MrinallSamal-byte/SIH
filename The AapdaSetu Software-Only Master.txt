The AapdaSetu Software-Only Master Plan: Web Application & AI Ecosystem

1. Web-Based Emergency SOS Alert Hub
The Real-World Problem: Victims in panic cannot navigate complex multi-step forms.
The Software Solution: A clean web intake interface that captures transcripts, victim demographics (age, medical conditions), and location coordinates to dispatch immediate priority alerts.

2. Explainable AI Urgency Triage Engine (apps/ai-engine/app/triage.py)
The Real-World Problem: Control rooms receive thousands of calls, leading to deadly dispatch delays.
The Software Solution: Scoring logic that evaluates transcript keywords and demographic vulnerability (elderly, infants, pre-existing conditions) to categorize requests into RED (Critical), YELLOW (High), and GREEN (Normal) tiers.

3. Disaster-Aware Dynamic Routing Engine (Leaflet.js)
The Real-World Problem: Standard GPS maps lead evacuees into newly flooded underpasses or collapsed bridges.
The Software Solution: Leaflet.js GIS map displaying real-time hazard polygons and dynamically calculating safe evacuation routes around flooded zones.

4. AI-Powered Psychological First Aid (PFA) Chatbot (apps/ai-engine/app/pfa_chatbot.py)
The Real-World Problem: Panic and trauma cause survivors to make poor decisions while waiting for rescue.
The Software Solution: Conversational AI bot delivering step-by-step 4-second breathing exercises, 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding instructions, and survival guidance.

5. Crowdsourced AI Damage Assessment (apps/ai-engine/app/damage_assessment.py)
The Real-World Problem: Property compensation takes months due to slow surveys and fraud.
The Software Solution: Citizens submit property damage photos; the system verifies EXIF metadata, checks pHash duplicates, and grades structural damage severity (FULLY_DESTROYED, MAJOR_STRUCTURAL_DAMAGE, MINOR_DAMAGE).

6. Satellite Imagery AI Flood Mapping (apps/ai-engine/app/satellite_flood_mapping.py)
The Real-World Problem: Ground visibility is zero during massive floods.
The Software Solution: Ingests Sentinel-1 SAR satellite metadata to generate real-time GeoJSON flood extent polygons indicating water depth and affected administrative sectors.

7. Low-Bandwidth WebRTC Telemedicine Portal
The Real-World Problem: Isolated medical emergencies cannot wait for physical rescue.
The Software Solution: Web-based telemedicine consultation queue connecting victims with remote doctors via low-bitrate video/audio links.

8. Algorithmic Skill-Matching Volunteer Dispatch
The Real-World Problem: Skilled volunteers (Doctors, Swimmers) are assigned to manual labor.
The Software Solution: Rescuer portal matching verified volunteer skills with nearest emergency tasks using spatial proximity.

9. Multi-Agency Incident Command System (ICS Dashboard)
The Real-World Problem: Agencies work in silos without shared situational awareness.
The Software Solution: Shared Leaflet.js command map rendering incident locations, agency geofences, shelter status, and volunteer distributions.

10. Zero User-Side Authentication Policy
To ensure zero friction during emergency operations, no user login or account creation is required for citizens to trigger SOS alerts, view safe routes, interact with the PFA chatbot, or report damages.
